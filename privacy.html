derma privacy policy

Version 1 · Effective October 5, 2026

derma reads visible skin attributes from a photo of your face and suggests a skincare routine. This policy explains what that involves. The short version:

  • Your photo is used only to read your skin. We never store it. Our analysis provider deletes it within 24 hours at most.
  • Your results and your answers stay on your phone.
  • Our server keeps an anonymous ID, how many reads you've used, and your agreement to the photo analysis. Nothing else.
  • You can delete everything at any time in the app: Settings, then Delete my data.
  • derma is for adults (18 and over). It gives cosmetic guidance, not medical advice.

Who we are

derma is run by Michael Kwon, Vaughan, Ontario, Canada ("we", "us"). The person responsible for privacy is Michael Kwon. For any question or request about your information, email derma.privacy@gmail.com.

Your face photo

A photo of your face can be biometric information, so we treat it with extra care.

  • Only with your agreement. Before your first read, the app explains what happens to your photo and asks you to agree. No photo is analysed without it, and the server checks this before every read.
  • One purpose. Your photo is used only to read your skin for you, then deleted. It isn't used to identify you, and no facial recognition is used.
  • Checked on your phone first. The app checks the photo's light and framing on your phone. Only a cropped photo of your face is sent, securely, and the app deletes its own copy as soon as the read is done.
  • Deleted quickly. We don't store your photo. Our analysis provider, Perfect Corp, processes it and keeps it for no more than 24 hours. We ask for it to be deleted as soon as each read is done.
  • Never sold or shared for any other purpose.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: in the app, go to Settings, then Delete my data. This deletes your account on our server (read counts and agreement included) and the results and answers on your phone. It also withdraws your agreement to the photo analysis.
  • Withdraw your agreement: deleting your data, as above, withdraws it. No further photos are analysed.
  • Access or correction: your results and answers are on your phone, where you can see them. For anything else, email us. We reply within 30 days.
  • Complaints: please contact us first. In Canada you can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or in Quebec to the Commission d'accès à l'information.

If you live in the United States, you may have extra rights under your state's law, such as rights to access or delete information and to withdraw consent. Email us to use them; we won't treat you differently for doing so.

Age

derma is for adults (18 and over). The app asks for your year of birth before you start and stops if you're under 18. We keep only whether you are 18 or over, never your year of birth, and that stays on your phone.

Not medical advice

derma describes how your skin looks. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. See a dermatologist about anything painful, changing or spreading.

Security

Information travels between the app and our server over encrypted connections. Our database is closed to the app: only our own server code can read or change it. No method is perfectly secure, but we keep what we hold to a minimum, so there is little to expose.

Changes to this policy

If we change what happens to your photo or to the information we keep, we'll update this page, change its version number, and ask you to agree again in the app before your next read.
