1. Summary

  • We use a photo of your face only to analyse visible skin attributes for you, and only after you give express consent. We do not store the photo. Our analysis provider deletes it within 24 hours.
  • Your skin results and your answers to the in-app questions are stored on your device, not on our servers.
  • Our servers hold an anonymous identifier, a count of the analyses you have used, and a record of your consent.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or precise location. We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or use facial recognition.
  • You can delete your data at any time in the app, under Settings > Delete my data.
  • The Services are intended only for adults aged 18 or older.

2. Scope and definitions

This policy applies to personal information we process through the Services. It does not apply to third-party services you access separately, such as the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

In this policy:

  • "Personal information" means information about an identifiable individual, and includes "personal data" and "personal information" as defined in applicable law.
  • "Face photo" means a photograph of your face taken in the app for skin analysis.
  • "Biometric information" means a face photo and any information derived from it that could be considered a biometric identifier, biometric information or biometric data under applicable law. We treat all face photos as biometric information.
  • "Skin results" means the scores and descriptions of visible skin attributes (such as oiliness, texture, redness and blemishes) returned by the analysis.

4. How we use information

We use personal information only for the following purposes:

  • to analyse visible skin attributes from your face photo and show you the results;
  • to tailor your results and suggested skincare routine to your answers;
  • to apply daily usage limits and protect the Services from abuse;
  • to record and verify your consent;
  • to diagnose and fix technical problems and keep the Services secure;
  • to respond to your questions and requests; and
  • to comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, rent it, use it for targeted or cross-context behavioural advertising, or use your face photos or skin results to train machine-learning models. If we want to use personal information for a new purpose, we will ask for your consent first where required by law.

5. Face photos and biometric information

This section is our written policy on biometric information and our retention schedule for it.

  1. Purpose. We collect a face photo only to analyse visible skin attributes and return your skin results. We do not use face photos to identify you, verify your identity or recognise you across sessions, and no facial recognition technology is used.
  2. Consent first. Before your first analysis, the app tells you what happens to your photo and asks for your express written consent, given by ticking a box and confirming. No face photo is sent for analysis without it, and our servers check for your consent before every analysis.
  3. How it is processed. The app first checks the photo's lighting and framing on your device. It then crops the photo to your face and sends it over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards it to our analysis provider, Perfect Corp. Our server holds the photo in memory only for the duration of the request and never writes it to storage. The app deletes its own temporary copy once the request is complete.
  4. Retention and destruction. We do not retain face photos. After each analysis our server asks Perfect Corp to delete the photo, and under its policy Perfect Corp deletes uploaded photos within 24 hours. In all cases, biometric information is permanently destroyed when the purpose for collecting it has been satisfied, and never later than 3 years after your last interaction with the Services, or any shorter period required by law.
  5. No sale or profit. We do not sell, lease, trade or otherwise profit from biometric information.
  6. Limited disclosure. We disclose face photos only to Perfect Corp, solely to perform the analysis, or where required by law or valid legal process.
  7. Protection. We protect biometric information using at least the same standard of care we use for other confidential and sensitive information, as described in section 10.

6. Consent

We rely on your consent to process personal information. Using a face photo for skin analysis requires your express consent, which we record. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data (see section 11). Withdrawing consent stops all future analyses but does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew it. Without consent to face photo processing, the skin analysis feature cannot be used.

If we change how face photos or other personal information are handled, we will ask for your consent again before your next analysis.

10. Security

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information, including:

  • encryption of information in transit;
  • access controls that prevent the app and its users from reading the database directly, with access limited to our server code;
  • keeping service credentials on our servers, never in the app; and
  • collecting as little information as possible, and keeping most of it on your device.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a security incident involving your personal information creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify the relevant regulators and, where possible, affected individuals, as required by law. Because we do not collect contact details, notice to individuals may be given in the app or on this page.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we hold about you and how it is used;
  • access a copy of it, including in a structured, commonly used format;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • delete your information;
  • withdraw consent; and
  • complain to a privacy regulator.

11.1 How to use your rights

  • Delete your data and withdraw consent: in the app, go to Settings > Delete my data. This permanently deletes your anonymous account and its usage counts and consent record from our servers, removes your skin results and answers from your device, and withdraws your consent.
  • See or correct your information: your skin results and answers are stored on your device and shown in the app. You can change your answers by deleting your data and starting again.
  • Any other request: email derma.privacy@gmail.com.

11.2 Verification and response

Because our servers do not hold your name or contact details, we may be unable to link an email request to your anonymous account. In-app deletion is the most reliable way to act on your server data. If we need more information to verify a request, we will tell you what we need and use it only for that purpose. We respond within 30 days, or within any shorter period required by law, and will explain if we need more time. We do not charge for requests unless permitted by law, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

You may use an authorised agent to make a request on your behalf where the law allows. We may ask the agent for proof of authorisation.

14. Age requirement

The Services are intended only for adults aged 18 or older. The app asks for your year of birth before you start and does not continue if you are under 18; only the outcome is kept, on your device. 15. Automated processing

Skin results are produced automatically by our analysis provider, and product suggestions are produced automatically by fixed rules that match your skin results and answers to product characteristics. Each suggestion comes with a short explanation of why it was chosen. These suggestions are cosmetic recommendations only and do not produce legal or similarly significant effects. Contact us if you have questions about how they are produced.

16. Not medical advice

The Services provide cosmetic information about the visible appearance of your skin. They do not diagnose, treat or prevent any disease or medical condition, and are not a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Consult a dermatologist about anything painful, changing or spreading.
