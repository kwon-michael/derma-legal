Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Version 1 · Effective date: October 5, 2026 · Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy applies to "consumer health data" as defined in Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada Senate Bill 370 and the Connecticut Data Privacy Act. It supplements our Privacy Policy, which also applies. derma is operated by Michael Kwon, Vaughan, Ontario, Canada.

1. Consumer health data we collect

derma provides cosmetic skincare information. Some information may be considered consumer health data because it relates to the condition or appearance of your skin:

  • face photos taken for skin analysis, which may also be biometric data;
  • skin results describing visible attributes such as oiliness, texture, redness and blemishes; and
  • your answers about your skin goals, how your skin feels and how it reacts to products; and
  • optional daily check-ins about how your skin feels, with notes you choose, such as sleep, stress, a new product or your period.

2. Sources

We collect this information directly from you, through the app. Skin results are generated from your face photo by our analysis provider.

3. Purposes

We collect and use consumer health data only to provide the skin analysis and skincare suggestions you request, with your consent. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.

4. How it is stored

Skin results, answers and check-ins are stored on your device only. Face photos are not stored by us and are deleted by our analysis provider within 24 hours. Our servers hold no consumer health data, only an anonymous identifier, usage counts and your consent record.

5. Sharing

We share consumer health data only with our processor Perfect Corp., which analyses face photos on our behalf, and only to provide the Services. We do not share consumer health data with any other third party, and we do not sell it.

6. Consent

We collect consumer health data only with your consent, which the app asks for before your first skin analysis. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app, under Settings > Delete my data.

7. Your rights

You have the right to confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, to access it, including a list of the third parties and affiliates it was shared with, to withdraw consent, and to have it deleted. Because your skin results and answers are stored on your device, you can see them in the app and delete them under Settings > Delete my data. For any other request, email derma.privacy@gmail.com. We respond within 45 days, and may extend this by a further 45 days where reasonably necessary, in which case we will tell you why.

If we decline your request, you may appeal by emailing us with "Appeal" in the subject line. We will respond to your appeal within 45 days. If your appeal is denied, you may contact the Attorney General of your state.
